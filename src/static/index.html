About this tool

Chagas disease affects approximately 6–7 million people worldwide, concentrated in Latin America. Diagnosis through electrocardiographic analysis is a common clinical approach, but requires specialist interpretation that is not always available in endemic regions.

This tool provides automated preliminary classification from a 12-lead ECG signal in WFDB format. It is intended as a screening aid for qualified medical professionals — not a replacement for clinical judgment or confirmatory testing.

Research use only. Chagas-ECG-Net is not a certified medical device and has not been validated for clinical deployment. Predictions must be interpreted by a qualified healthcare professional. Do not use as the sole basis for diagnostic or treatment decisions.
